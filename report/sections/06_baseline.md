# 6. Baseline LLM implementation

The baseline (E1) asks each model one plain question about the customer's message (Appendix A.1). The prompt has no intent list, no output schema and no examples, and decoding is greedy with at most 48 new tokens. The same five evaluation queries, from five different intents, go to GPT-2 [3], a base model with no instruction tuning or chat template, and to Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct [4], the model used in every later experiment. GPT-2 replies are shortened here; line breaks are shown as "/".

| Query | Gold intent | GPT-2 | Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct |
|---|---|---|---|
| I need to activate my card can you do that now? | `activate_my_card` | I need to activate my card can you do that now? / What does the customer want? Answer in one short sentence. / Answer: I need to activate my card … | The customer wants to activate their card immediately. |
| I have been charged a fee for paying with card | `card_payment_fee_charged` | I have been charged a fee for paying with card / What does the customer want? Answer in one short sentence. / What does the customer want? … | The customer wants to understand why they were charged a fee for using a card to pay. |
| Is it possible to edit my personal details on the app? | `edit_personal_details` | Yes, it is possible to edit your personal details on the app. / What does the customer want? Answer in one short sentence. / … | The customer wants to know if they can update their personal information on the app. |
| I tried taking money from the ATM but no money came out, but the transaction is still pending? How is this possible? | `pending_cash_withdrawal` | I am a customer. I am a customer. I am a customer. I am a customer. … | The customer is confused about why their ATM transaction is pending even though no money has come out. |
| My top-up was revoked. | `top_up_reverted` | I'm sorry. / What does the customer want? Answer in one short sentence. / Answer: I'm sorry. … | The customer wants to understand why their top-up was revoked and possibly how to resolve the issue. |

**What the baseline shows.** GPT-2 never answers the question. In four of five cases it continues the text pattern instead: it repeats the query or the prompt's own instruction lines until it runs out of tokens. On the ATM query it gets stuck repeating a single phrase. Its one fluent reply ("Yes, it is possible …") answers as the bank rather than describing what the customer wants. This is what a model trained only to predict the next token does without instruction tuning. Qwen gives a correct one-sentence summary of the customer's need for all five queries, so the instruction-tuned model understands the requests. None of its replies is one of the 77 intent labels, though, and nothing in its output can be checked or passed to a tool. The rest of the system addresses that gap: the intent list and JSON schema in the prompt (§7), and validation and repair (§10).
